⚜ How Booking Works Here

You pick the artist. Not the other way around.

Step One

Tell us what you're picturing

References, a placement, a rough size — even just "something for my late father." No account, no forms, just a conversation.

⚜
Step Two

We match you to the right hand

Portrait or memorial work goes to Bolo. Dark, illustrative, or Florida-inspired pieces go to Sebastian. You're never guessing which artist fits your idea.

⚜
Step Three

Your artist locks the design

Every custom piece is approved by the artist tattooing it — size, placement, color vs. black-and-grey, budget — before a date is confirmed.

⚜
Step Four

Chair time, no rushing

Multi-session pieces are scheduled around the work, not the calendar — sterile setup, single-use needles, and the time it takes to get it right.
